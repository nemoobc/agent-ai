# Incident Response

## When to use
Triage active incidents, contain damage, recover systems, communicate status, and conduct blameless postmortems.

## Key rules
- Acknowledge within 5 minutes; assign incident commander immediately
- Communicate early and often — status page, Slack, email
- Contain first, investigate second, remediate third
- Preserve evidence; don't destroy logs or state during recovery
- Postmortem within 48 hours; blameless, action-oriented

## Severity levels
- **SEV1**: Complete outage or data loss — all hands
- **SEV2**: Major feature degraded — dedicated responder
- **SEV3**: Minor issue — next business day
